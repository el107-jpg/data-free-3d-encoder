Add distillation into a changed decoder form (scripts/distill_data_free.py)

arm_planner/data_free/distill.py trains a student decoder to reproduce a
teacher's field, value and slope, at fresh random codes and the anchor codes,
on points half uniform and half near the teacher's surface. The script loads
the teacher's checkpoint with changed settings (e.g. sphere_base=False) as the
student, so it starts from the teacher's weights, anchor codes and
multipliers, distils, clears the optimiser state and saves a checkpoint that
training continues from. Used to give the base-free decoder a start with the
fixed-base model's shapes and code directions.

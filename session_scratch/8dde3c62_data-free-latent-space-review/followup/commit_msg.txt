Anchor build: save every eligible object, take the size target and base radius as arguments, refuse to overwrite

--save-eligible writes eligible.pt (grids of all eligible objects with a flag for the chosen ones) so objects that were not chosen can serve as held-out targets. --cov-trace-target and --base-radius let a build follow a non-default decoder configuration. An output folder that already holds anchors.pt is left alone.

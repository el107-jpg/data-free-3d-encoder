Reading the numbers

Variance
Total shape variety over random codes. Higher is better. Grows with shape size, so compare at the same size.
Dirs
Solid directions: code directions that move the surface by more than 0.02 per unit step.
Clean
Fresh random codes out of 128 that decode to one solid piece with no cavity or handle.
Anchors
Mean overlap (0–1) between each anchor object and the decoder's shape at its code.
Unseen fit
Mean overlap when a code is fitted to each of 225 Thingi objects the model never trained on. The direct test of coverage.
Capped-follow
The variety push acts at the surface, and the consistency term's weight is capped at 20 so it can't freeze the shapes.

- Raw gradient share by layer is misleading under Adam: the variety gradient is ~97% in the final layer, but the actual Adam step (`analysis_scripts/step_by_layer.py`, 14 Nov 2026) gets only 8–20% of its shape change from the final layer and 45–60% from the side network. Judge "which layer does the work" with that script, not with gradient shares. Weight normalisation (W1) made things worse.
- The logged "live" direction count depends on the field's current error level; judge direction counts with the per-code spectrum (`shell_variance.py` + `local_spectra.py`), not the log.
